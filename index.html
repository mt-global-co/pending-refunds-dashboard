Proposed policy ladder draft — offer in this order, escalate only if declined

  1. Step 1 — Keep the item, partial refund. Offer 40% where the complaint is quality, sizing or minor damage and the item is usable. No return shipping, customer keeps the goods.
  2. Step 2 — Exchange. If the partial is declined and the issue is sizing or a wrong item, offer a replacement in the correct size before offering money back. Keeps the sale.
  3. Step 3 — Higher partial. Escalate to 50–60% before conceding a full refund.
  4. Step 4 — Full refund. Automatic where the order never shipped, went out of stock, was lost in transit, or is a duplicate — a partial is not defensible in those cases.
